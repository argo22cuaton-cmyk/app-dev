# Moana (2016)

**Moana** is my absolute favorite movie — a vibrant, heartfelt Disney animated adventure that perfectly blends stunning visuals, unforgettable music, and a powerful message about identity and courage.

> "I am Moana of Motunui. You will board my boat, sail across the sea, and restore the heart of Te Fiti."  
> — Moana

### Why I Love It
- Beautiful Polynesian-inspired animation and culture
- Incredible soundtrack by Lin-Manuel Miranda, Opetaia Foa’i, and Mark Mancina
- Strong female lead who doesn’t need a prince to save the day
- Hilarious sidekick (looking at you, *Heihei*)
